## Doctoral Dissertation

My doctoral dissertation focuses on **international political communication**, examining how, under the dual forces of great-power competition and digital technology, the political nature of international communication is being reactivated and comprehensively foregrounded under contemporary conditions. The study identifies three contemporary manifestations of the politicization of international communication and explores the profound implications of this process for the global communication order, cultural narratives, and ethical norms — offering a new theoretical perspective, at the intersection of political communication and international relations, for understanding contemporary practices of international communication.
