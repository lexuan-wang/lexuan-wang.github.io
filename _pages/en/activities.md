## Panel on "International Political Communication in the Age of Artificial Intelligence"

Organized the panel "International Political Communication in the Age of Artificial Intelligence" during the 19th Annual Conference of the Chinese Community of Political Science and International Relations. Invited young doctoral students from Peking University, China Foreign Affairs University, East China Normal University, Beijing Language and Culture University, Communication University of China, and other institutions to discuss the re-politicization of international communication, AI governance norms, strategic narratives and technological competition, and Confucian thought and international organizations. Discussants included Associate Professor Zhang Chang (Communication University of China), Mr. Song Guoxin (Jilin University), and Ms. Wu Huanqiong (Beijing Language and Culture University).
